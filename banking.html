Banking Basics

Tips

1. Choose a bank with no monthly fees and no minimum balance — many online banks offer this completely free

2. Always keep a $20–50 buffer above what you plan to spend — overdraft fees can be $35 per transaction

3. Use only your own bank's ATMs — out-of-network fees add up to $3–5 every single withdrawal

4. Set up low-balance alerts on your phone so you're never caught off guard when funds get low

5. Never share your PIN, card number, or online banking password — not even with people you trust
